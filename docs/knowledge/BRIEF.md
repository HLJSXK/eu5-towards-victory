## Summary

Towards Victory adds 6 generalized victory paths to EU5: Conquest, Prosperity, Trade, Diplomatic, Cultural, and Scientific. Each path has 5 milestone nodes that grant permanent buffs when reached. Progress is displayed via situation panels; milestones trigger popup events that notify the player and deliver the reward.

The **Conquest Victory** path (征服胜利) is fully implemented: 5 triggers, 5 reward modifiers, 5 events, and localization in English and Simplified Chinese. Score = direct locations + 0.5 × subject-or-below locations; thresholds at 200 / 400 / 600 / 1200 / 2500.

The **Prosperity Victory** path (繁荣胜利) is fully implemented: 5 triggers, 5 reward modifiers, 5 events, and localization in English and Simplified Chinese. Score = (total_population + Σdev + country_tax_base) × 50 / (N + 50) × (1 + stability/100) × (gov_power/100), where N = num_owned_locations and gov_power = legitimacy/republican_tradition/devotion/tribal_cohesion/horde_unity by government type. The 50N/(N+50) effective scale term provides stronger diminishing returns on country size. Updated yearly; thresholds at 2,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 / 7,000 / 11,000 (provisional, calibrate after playtesting).

**Prosperity main mechanic:** Prosperity M1 creates the **Engineering Department IO** (`tv_engineering_department`), a non-unique founder-only IO with no parliament tab and no automatic leader election. Its appointed character leader is the **Great Engineer**, stored on the leader country as `tv_great_engineer_char` and managed by generated Appoint/Remove/Change leader actions. Wonder Construction implements **Conception, Debate, Survey, Organization, Logistics, Construction, Ceremony, and Finalization** for the initial universal wonder set: Sacred Mountain Complex, Triumphal Axis City, and Great Port. Appointing a Great Engineer rebuilds the country's feasible proposal deck from owned terrain/city/port conditions, rolls or restores that engineer's proposal, and stores it on the character so switching back does not refresh it. The player can accept a proposal to lock the wonder and enter debate, refute it for 10 Prestige, or bribe for a reroll; rejected proposals are removed from the current deck. Debate uses the Engineering Department IO variable `tv_wonder_domestic_support` (0-200): estate demands for Nobles, Burghers, and Clergy can be accepted for +20 support or rerolled by refutation/bribery, monthly debate events can swing support by +/-20, and the debate can end at 100 support. After debate ends, support decays by 1 per month until the wonder is finalized; reaching 0 before finalization forces project collapse. Abandoning or forced collapse applies upper-estate satisfaction penalties, removes the Great Engineer, costs 20 Prestige, and resets wonder project state.

After debate, the player starts a location-select survey through `tv_wonder_start_survey`. Valid sites depend on the locked wonder type (Sacred Mountain: hills/plateau/mountains; Triumphal Axis: city/megalopolis; Great Port: port). Survey progress is the Engineering Department IO variable `tv_wonder_survey_progress`; each monthly tick adds 0.1 times the Great Engineer's MIL and can fire one survey event from a single random roll. The country tracks the current survey site and three visible competence values: `tv_wonder_scale_competence`, `tv_wonder_logistics_competence`, and `tv_wonder_organization_competence`, initialized at 0 when a new survey begins. At survey start, hidden target competence values are precomputed from 25 base competence, each field's random 0-25 roll, the site preference bonus, and up to 10 from the Great Engineer's ADM; the visible competence values then rise by fixed monthly increments until completion. Monthly survey events can still raise or lower one visible competence by 5, or offer resource tradeoffs for +5. Site preference bonuses now use: Sacred Mountain topography/vegetation (mountains +15, plateau +7.5, hills +0, forest +10, woods +5); Triumphal Axis development (0-100 maps linearly to 0-20) plus +5 for megalopolis; Great Port harbor capacity (`harbor_suitability`, 0-100% maps linearly to 0-25, capped above 100%). Completed survey results are stored on the location per wonder type; selecting an already surveyed site restores the stored result instead of restarting progress. Competence maps to scale/logistics/organization tiers from 1 to 6 using thresholds 20/40/55/70/85. `tv_wonder_select_construction_site` now confirms the completed survey site, grants 0/50/100 domestic support from the average result (<50 / 50-74 / >=75), and unlocks organization, logistics, and construction.

Three low-cost support buildings drive the active construction loop: `tv_wonder_labor_camp` (site-only, up to 5 levels per scale tier, 1,000 laborers per level, +2 migration attraction), `tv_wonder_material_depot` (site-only, up to 1 level per scale tier, 60,000 material capacity per level), and `tv_wonder_material_dispatch_point` (one per owned location, contributes 100 materials per month). Completed units and part progress persist across project completion or collapse so a later expansion can continue from the finished baseline. Once `tv_wonder_level >= 1`, the player may declare construction complete, which destroys the site depot, locks further construction, awards +20 support, and unlocks ceremonies. Each wonder has three ceremony variants: Sacred Mountain can become a Golden Sanctuary through royal sacrifice, an Ascetic Mountain after a one-year meditation penalty, or a Theocratic Center by converting the cabinet to clergy; Triumphal Axis can become a Sun King Axis by receiving five neighboring emissaries, a Military City after a victory parade and winning a war, or a Civic Capital after a one-year production-efficiency festival; Great Port can become a Golden Trade Port through five scaled-gold auctions, an Imperial Naval Port with at least 30 heavy ships, or a Free Trade Metropolis after a one-year trade-income festival. Finalization constructs the matching event-only local wonder building at the selected site, applies the base wonder-level country modifier and the chosen ceremony country modifier permanently, and then stops support decay. Because `capital_country_modifier` only applies when a building is in the capital, finalized wonder buildings carry only local effects while national effects are applied by scripted country modifiers. The custom IO panel hides the Members tab; its overview tab contains pipeline, conception, and debate; its resolutions tab contains Survey plus Organization/Logistics; and its custom tab contains Construction plus Ceremony controls.

1. **6 Victory Situations** — One situation (`tv_victory_situation`) displays all 6 victory paths in a single panel with a top-level manual recovery button (`tv_reinitialize_mod`), a flavor text introduction (Section 1), per-path progress bars and milestone circles (Section 2), and a global leaderboard (Section 3).
2. **Milestone Events** — Popup country events per milestone. All 30 events implemented across 6 paths: Conquest (`tv.conquest.1`–`5`), Prosperity (`tv.prosperity.1`–`5`), Trade (`tv.trade.1`–`5`), Diplomatic (`tv.diplomatic.1`–`5`), Cultural (`tv.cultural.1`–`5`), and Scientific (`tv.science.1`–`5`).
3. **Permanent Rewards** — All 30 milestone rewards are permanent static modifiers (`days = -1`); no time-limited buffs. All 6 paths fully implemented.
4. **Diplomatic Victory Points** (`tv_diplomatic_victory_points`) — Permanently accumulated via `on_royal_marriage` (+3 DVP each party) and `on_winning_war` (+5 DVP to winner).
5. **Cultural Influence Points** (`tv_cultural_influence_points`) — Accumulated via `on_work_of_art_created` (+3 CIP to `root.owner`), `monthly_country_pulse` (+1 CIP/month), and persistent local exhibition rewards from the Arts Exhibition IO.
6. **Scientific Technology Score** (`tv_science_score`) — Monthly snapshot of `num_of_advances_researched`; thresholds 100 / 150 / 200 / 300 / 400.
7. **Academy of Sciences Building Chain** — Five capital buildings (`tv_academy_of_sciences_1`–`5`) gate Scientific Victory milestones. Each requires the previous phase built and the corresponding score threshold; `on_built` immediately triggers `tv_check_science_milestones_effect`. Building category: `cultural_category`; visible in all capitals from game start. Phase I no longer fires a separate Academy popup; Scientific Victory M1 creates the **Academy of Sciences IO** (`tv_academy_of_sciences`) inside its reward effect and shows a custom tooltip pointing to the Research Mechanism in the International Organization interface. Membership flag: `tv_academy_io_member` (country variable set to 1 on join). IO leader (Chief Scientist) = appointed non-artist character stored on leader_country as `tv_academy_leader_char`; managed via the Appoint/Remove/Change Leader actions in `tv_io_leader_actions.txt`. `leader_change_trigger_type = none`; `international_organization_chooses_new_leader` is deliberately NOT called — it triggers the vanilla leader-election process, violating the no-elections design. The Academy has a vanilla IO Laws tab but no Parliament tab; it starts with no initial law policies, while `tv_science_factions` and `tv_academy_leadership` law changes keep `requires_vote = yes`. `tv_academy_leader_gender_law` can remove the female-candidate filter under `tv_academy_leader_gender_unrestricted_policy` once that policy is enacted.
8. **Research Mechanism (Frontier-Tech Shortcut)** — Full four-subprocess cycle that auto-researches one of 20 active Frontier advances via `research_advance` on cycle completion. The advances remain normally researchable via the vanilla tech tree — the Research Mechanism is an *extra* unlock path, not a gate. Sub-process A: monthly random interest events (~10%; 5 funded successes needed; event namespace `tv_research`; each success costs dynamic gold via `change_gold_effect = { scale = -2 }`). Sub-process B: passive prerequisite check; institution-root targets require at least one owned location matching the corresponding institution's plausible spawn conditions with geography/region and existing-institution gates removed, while non-institution-root targets still complete automatically. Sub-process C: IO variable `tv_research_c_progress` auto-accumulates via `monthly_change`; completed by `monthly_effect`. Once A/B/C are all complete, `tv_research.19` pops once as a no-effect reminder that concentrated research can begin. Sub-process D: IO variable `tv_research_d_progress` auto-accumulates via the appointed leader's effective ADM (base ADM plus Great Scientist trait bonus), applies `tv_concentrated_research_penalty` (−20% research speed) while active, triggers unlock on completion, and grants/upgrades that Chief Scientist's Great Scientist trait. Three research generic actions: `tv_request_research_target`, `tv_abandon_research_target`, `tv_start_concentrated_research`; abandoning a proposed or active target costs 10 Prestige and has no cooldown, with abandon UI gated behind 10 Prestige. Lifecycle managed by scripted effects in `tv_research_subprocess_effects.txt`; conditions in `tv_research_subprocess_triggers.txt`; the Academy overview displays B's current conditions via `ShowTriggerConditions('tv_research_check_b_trigger', PlayerScope.Self)`. **20 active Frontier advances:** 1=printing_press(A3), 2=iron_working(A1), 4=gunpowder(A2), 5=blast_furnace(A3), 6=scientific_experimentation(A4), 7=scientific_revolution(A5), 8=manufactories(A5), 9=industrialization(A6), 10=vaccination(A6), 11=renaissance(A2), 12=banking(A2), 13=professional_armies(A2), 14=new_world(A3), 15=pike_and_shot(A3), 16=confessionalism(A4), 17=global_trade(A4), 18=artillery_institution(A4), 19=military_revolution(A5), 20=enlightenment(A6), 21=levee_en_masse(A6). Value 3 is retired from the old anatomy(A2) target and is intentionally not reused. Each is listed in `data/locked_advances.yaml` with `vanilla_advance_id` and `age` fields; the `tv_unlock_advance_<id>_effect` (set_variable + `research_advance = advance_type:<vanilla_id>`) is generated by `scripts/gen_locked_advances.py`. Target proposal uses strict-earliest-age priority: each candidate is excluded if `has_advance = vanilla_id` is true OR if any earlier-age Frontier advance is still unresearched.
9. **IO Leader Management** — Unified character-leader pattern across five TV character roles (Arts Exhibition, Diplomatic Alliance, Academy of Sciences, Governor's House Grand General, Engineering Department Great Engineer). Each IO uses `leader_type = character` + `leader_change_trigger_type = none` + `leader_change_method = none` + a `leader = { leader_country ?= { var:tv_<io>_leader_char ?= { add_to_list = leaders } } }` block, with Governor's House using `tv_conquest_general_char` and Engineering Department using `tv_great_engineer_char`. Fifteen generic actions (5 roles × 3 actions) in `tv_io_leader_actions.txt`: Appoint, Remove, Change. Each action sets or clears the country variable only — `international_organization_chooses_new_leader` is deliberately NOT called (it triggers the vanilla leader-election process, violating the no-elections design). All TV IOs have `unique = no`; never use `international_organization:type_name` syntax for them — use `every_international_organizations_member_of = { limit = { international_organization_type = international_organization_type:<io_type> } ... }` instead. Arts Exhibition filters candidates by `is_artist = yes`; Academy and Engineering Department exclude artists; Diplomatic Alliance accepts any non-ruler/heir/consort alive adult character; Grand General additionally excludes female characters and artists. **Character title prefixes (Arts Exhibition + Academy + Governor's House + Engineering Department):** Appointing/removing leaders also adds/removes zero-stat character modifiers (`tv_chief_artist_role_modifier` / `tv_chief_scientist_role_modifier` / `tv_grand_general_role_modifier` / `tv_great_engineer_role_modifier`). These modifiers trigger title prefixes via generated `src/in_game/common/customizable_localization/character_title.txt` (copied from vanilla and patched by `scripts/in_game/common/customizable_localization/gen_character_title.py`) — the appointed character's name displays as "Chief Artist [Name]", "Chief Scientist [Name]", "Grand General [Name]", or "Great Engineer [Name]" (首席艺术家/首席科学家/大将军/大工程师). Modifier definitions are in `src/in_game/common/static_modifiers/tv_io_role_modifiers.txt`. The Diplomatic Alliance leader has no title modifier.
10. **Governor's House IO** (`tv_govhouse`): non-unique IO created by the Conquest Victory M1 reward (200 locations). **Governor system:** characters appointed by the player to govern EU5 regions. Governor bonuses scale dynamically with character attributes, but the exact local modifier is selected by the three **Local Governor Functions** IO laws. Administrative Function options: `tv_governor_max_control` (`local_max_control`, adm × 0.001, default), `tv_governor_monthly_development` (`local_monthly_development`, adm × 0.00001), or `tv_governor_production_efficiency` (`local_production_efficiency`, adm × 0.001). Diplomatic Function options: `tv_governor_build_cost` (`local_build_buildings_cost`, −dip × 0.002, default), `tv_governor_pop_promotion_speed` (`local_pop_promotion_speed_modifier`, dip × 0.005), or `tv_governor_population_growth` (`local_population_growth`, dip × 0.00001). Military Function options: `tv_governor_unrest` (`local_unrest`, −mil × 0.002, default), `tv_governor_levy_size` (`local_army_levy_size_modifier`, mil × 0.002), or `tv_governor_fort_defense` (`local_defensive`, mil × 0.005). Data model: `tv_govhouse_region_list` is a persistent country variable list of region scopes rebuilt from the country's currently owned locations at IO creation and every monthly governor refresh; `tv_govhouse_region_display_list` is a parallel country variable list of representative owned location scopes used by the GUI to display one row per owned region; `tv_governor_list` remains a persistent country variable list of appointed governor character scopes. Each governor character has `tv_is_governor`, `tv_governed_region` (region scope), `tv_governed_region_capital` (representative owned location retained for compatibility), and pre-computed function display variables (`tv_gov_admin_bonus_type/value`, `tv_gov_dip_bonus_type/value`, `tv_gov_mil_bonus_type/value`). The representative display location mirrors the active governor and copied function values via `tv_govhouse_display_governor_char` plus `tv_govhouse_display_{admin/dip/mil}_bonus_{type/value}`. Monthly_effect in the IO calls `tv_apply_governor_bonuses_effect`, which refreshes modifiers for alive governors, prunes dead/orphaned/lost-region assignments, and then rebuilds the region/display lists; legacy `tv_governed_area` assignments are migrated/cleaned during refresh. Three region-row generic actions: `tv_appoint_governor` (UI passes `scope:target_1` region, select_trigger chooses `scope:target` character), `tv_remove_governor` (UI passes region and the effect finds the assigned governor), and `tv_change_governor` (region parameter + character selection, replacing any existing assignment); all require an alive Grand General in office. If the Grand General is dismissed or dies, existing governors remain appointed and continue refreshing, but no new governor can be appointed/changed/removed until a successor is appointed. **Grand General:** character leader stored as the independent country variable `tv_conquest_general_char`; managed via generated Appoint/Remove/Change IO leader actions and displayed in the IO header. `tv_govhouse_authority` IO variable (0-100, monthly decay × 0.0005, grows by Grand General's mil × 0.05). The Governor's House overview tab renders the display-location list: each row shows a region icon/name, right-aligned ADM/DIP/MIL function entries with formula tooltips, and the current governor as a standard `io_character_card`; Assign/Cancel/Change Governor buttons still pass the row's region scope. The dedicated Grand General tab shows the current variable-backed character, active training progress below the character card, authority value, Appoint/Change/Remove actions, and the existing Train Grand General action.

Regional Governor random events: `tv_govhouse_governor_events_monthly_pulse` runs through the generated `monthly_country_pulse` registry and only triggers if at least one living governor has an owned governed region. It uses a single monthly country-level roll with 180 no-event weight plus 20 event weights, giving a 10% total monthly chance. Events `tv_govhouse.100`-`.119` each pick one random eligible governor in `immediate`, save the governor, governed region, and a representative owned location as event scopes, then apply one-year region-wide location modifiers and/or immediate or one-year country effects. The 20-event set covers two-choice good events, one-option good events, two-choice tradeoff events, two-choice bad events, and one-option bad events.

Governor's House laws: the IO uses the vanilla IO Laws tab. Six generated law groups (`tv_govhouse_expedition_goal_law`, `tv_govhouse_governor_appointment_law`, `tv_govhouse_local_autonomy_law`, `tv_govhouse_religious_mission_law`, `tv_govhouse_frontier_military_law`, `tv_govhouse_imperial_integration_law`) each have four effect-bearing country policies. Three additional generated Local Governor Function laws (`tv_govhouse_administrative_function_law`, `tv_govhouse_diplomatic_function_law`, `tv_govhouse_military_function_law`) each have three policies and decide which ADM/DIP/MIL-scaled local modifier governors apply to their regions. All nine laws keep `requires_vote = yes`, `has_parliament = no` hides the separate IO Parliament tab, and the IO starts with no initial policies. There is no monthly default-law backfill.

Governor's House War Tent: requires an alive Grand General. The custom War Tent tab has three units: General Tactics, War Plan, and Main Actions. On war entry, `tv_govhouse_war_entry_effect` proposes one random tactic from the seven-tactic pool and fires `tv_govhouse.40`; the player approves or rejects it. While continuously at war, the monthly pulse has a 5% chance to propose a replacement tactic, excluding the tactic already active. Approved tactics are applied as country-scoped static modifiers and are cleared when the country leaves all wars or loses its Grand General; this approximates general tactics because the army-wide combat modifiers did not apply reliably from the stored character path. War plans are mutually exclusive: offensive plans select a target country and, while at war with that target, scale Siege Ability by progress x 0.2% and Discipline by progress x 0.1%; defensive plans need no target and, while in any defensive war, scale Fort Defense by progress x 0.2% and Military Tactics by progress x 0.005. On war entry, applicable offensive/defensive plan bonuses are activated through `tv_govhouse.41` / `tv_govhouse.42` events instead of being granted directly by the war-entry effect. Plan progress increases monthly by Grand General adm x 0.02 up to 100; aborting/changing target resets it, and peace with an offensive plan target aborts that plan.

8. **Global Victory Leaderboard** — Monthly ranking of the top 5 countries by their best single-path progress (0–100 bar value). Per-country: `tv_best_path` (0–5), `tv_best_progress_pct`, `tv_best_milestone`, `tv_best_score` computed via `tv_update_best_path_effect` in `monthly_country_pulse`. Situation stores rank-1..5 country references (`tv_rank_1_country`–`tv_rank_5_country`) via `ordered_in_global_list` cascading-exclusion pattern. Displayed in Section 3 with flag, country name, leading-path circle icon, milestone count, and score text.

Governor's House eligibility/death note: new governors and Grand Generals exclude female characters and artists at the selection/action-effect entry points. There is no monthly legacy-eligibility cleanup, to keep the IO monthly path focused on active governor bonus refresh. `tv_govhouse_governor_death` is registered under `on_character_death`, fires `tv_govhouse.1`, removes the dead governor from `tv_governor_list`, clears their region modifiers, and rebuilds the display lists immediately.

Grand General training note: `tv_train_grand_general` applies the one-year `tv_grand_general_training_modifier` country modifier defined in `src/in_game/common/static_modifiers/tv_govhouse_modifiers.txt` (+10% `court_spending_cost_modifier`), tracks `tv_gg_training_months` on the Grand General via monthly pulse, mirrors it to the country-side GUI display variable `tv_gg_training_progress`, resolves at 12 months through `tv_training_resolve_effect`, and routes to skill gain, trait-path upgrade, injury, or death events before clearing state.

Grand General death notification note: natural Grand General deaths trigger `tv_govhouse.2` from `tv_govhouse_char_death`; training-caused deaths set `tv_gg_training_death_notified` before `kill_character = this` so the already-shown training death event is not duplicated.

Governor's House balance-of-power backlash note: `tv_govhouse_power_balance` is an IO variable in the 0..1 range and is checked monthly by `tv_govhouse_negative_monthly_pulse` (registered through `data/pulse_registry.yaml`). `tv_govhouse_power_equilibrium` recalculates monthly from a -20% base, the Grand General's share of combined ruler/general abilities, and `tv_govhouse_power_consolidation`; `tv_govhouse_power_balance` then moves toward that equilibrium by up to 0.6% per month. Below 25% no backlash events fire. At 25%/50%/75% the pulse uses a single `random_list` roll with a 300 total weight so at most one backlash event can appear per month, and each eligible event has weight 1/300. Events `tv_govhouse.10`-`.13` are tier 1, `.20`-`.25` are tier 2, and `.30`-`.34` are tier 3. Every backlash event has exactly two options: accept the negative effect, or pay an alternative resource and call `tv_govhouse_reduce_power_balance_effect` to lower the balance by 5 percentage points. Tier 2 adds rare art/artist losses; tier 3 upgrades the local satisfaction loss to an area-wide effect and adds a cabinet crisis. The Governor's House GUI shows right-aligned fixed-width 25/50/75/100% markers and four warning rows below the Balance of Power bar for the <25, 25-49, 50-74, and 75-100 segments.

Grand General replacement cost note: generated Remove/Change Grand General actions call `tv_govhouse_pay_grand_general_replacement_cost_effect` before resetting `tv_govhouse_power_consolidation`; the cost is Government Power equal to `tv_govhouse_power_consolidation * 20`, so a fully consolidated Grand General costs 20 government power to remove or replace.

## IO Architecture Design Decisions

The character-led TV IOs (`tv_arts_exhibition`, `tv_diplomatic_alliance`, `tv_academy_of_sciences`, `tv_govhouse`, `tv_engineering_department`) share invariants that must never be broken.

### 1. leader_country = founding country, locked at creation

`on_creation = { set_leader_country = scope:actor }` runs once when the IO is created. `leader_country` is not reassigned by normal gameplay afterward; the only exception is the manual recovery action `tv_reinitialize_mod`, exposed at the top of the Victory Situation panel, which recreates missing victory-path IOs where possible and reassigns joined TV IO instances back to the current country. It controls IO permissions, `is_leader_of_international_organization` trigger, and `leader_country.modifier:` chained access in `monthly_change` scripted values.

### 2. No elections — leader_change_trigger_type = none is mandatory

All TV character-led IO type definitions keep `leader_change_trigger_type = none` and `leader_change_method = none`. These prevent the engine from reassigning `leader_country` automatically. `international_organization_chooses_new_leader` is **globally banned** from all TV IO-related code — this effect triggers the vanilla election process. Any call to it violates the no-elections design and risks silently reassigning `leader_country` to a different country.

### 3. Great person characters are independent country variables, not the vanilla ruler

The character displayed in the IO header is stored as a country variable on the `leader_country`:
- Arts Exhibition: `tv_arts_exhibition_leader_char`
- Diplomatic Alliance: `tv_diplomatic_alliance_leader_char`
- Academy of Sciences: `tv_academy_leader_char`
- Governor's House: `tv_conquest_general_char`
- Engineering Department: `tv_great_engineer_char`

These are set/cleared exclusively by the generated generic actions in `tv_io_leader_actions.txt`. The vanilla `leader_country.GetGovernment.GetRuler` and `appointed_leader.*` are different concepts and must not be used in TV IO calculations.

### 4. IO variable monthly_change uses chained character attribute access

All IO variable `monthly_change` scripted values that depend on the appointed great person must use `leader_country.var:tv_xxx_leader_char.attribute` chained access — never `appointed_leader.attribute`. Examples:
- CORRECT: `value = leader_country.var:tv_academy_leader_char.adm`
- FORBIDDEN: `value = appointed_leader.adm`

### 5. Header display uses blockoverride

All character-led IO panels use `blockoverride` on their header template to display the appointed character (read from the country variable via `GetPlayer.MakeScope.GetVariable(...).GetCharacter`). Never use the vanilla `GetLeaderCountry.GetGovernment.GetRulerOrRegent` accessor chain.

### 6. All TV IOs have unique = no

Each country pursuing the relevant victory path creates its own independent IO instance. The `international_organization:type_name` scope link is **invalid** for non-unique types. Always use `every_international_organizations_member_of = { limit = { international_organization_type = international_organization_type:<io_type> } ... }`.

## Quick Reference Rules

- **3-Step Rule**: Direct edit only if 100% certain → `reference_official_defines/` → `reference_game_files/` + `reference_mods/`
- **Mandatory Step 2/3** for: `blockoverride` names, `custom_tooltip` keys, `situation_card_common` structure, `location_rank:*`, modifier names, scripted triggers/effects, localization YAML encoding, GUI expressions
- **Verification format**: output `**Verification** — Step [2/3], Reference: file:line, Quote: "exact text"` before any code
- **Bug fix rule**: Replace syntax with verified form; do NOT remove the feature
- **Pre-test**: Run `python scripts/validate.py --changed` before launching game
- **Float precision**: EU5 truncates all floats at 5 decimal places; never emit 6+ dp in mod files
