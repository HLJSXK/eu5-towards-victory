Trade League monopoly tracking and actions are generated from the pure goods list in `data/trade_league_goods.yaml`. Monopoly tracking uses an annual global good refresh, yearly per-league full recalculation, and monthly slot-only recalculation. The annual global refresh writes keyed `global_variable_map` entries for each good index: global production, global consumption, representative origin/transit/consumer markets, representative static display metrics, and effective-market denominators. It also maintains three global lists per good: origin-effective markets whose production is at least 20% of the highest production market, transit-effective markets whose export is at least 20% of the highest export market, and consumer-effective markets whose demand is at least 20% of the highest demand market; zero-value markets are excluded. Origin and transit denominators are total export across their effective lists, and the consumer denominator is total import across its effective list. Representative markets are only detail-page decision support. The yearly Trade League pulse refreshes the global cache once per year through a global version/cooldown, then each eligible Trade League leader that has not seen that version recomputes every tracked good and refills monopoly slots. The monthly pulse recomputes only goods already stored in unlocked or previously-filled monopoly slots, using `every_in_global_list` over the effective market lists rather than scanning every world market. Origin and transit control divide member export from effective markets by the cached effective total export; consumer control divides member import from effective markets by the cached effective total import. The final `tv_trade_monopoly_level_pct_<good>` is the maximum of those three percentages, capped at 100, with `tv_trade_monopoly_type_<good>` set to 1 origin, 2 transit, or 3 consumer. `tv_trade_monopoly_stage_<good>` is 0 none, 1 potential at 25%, 2 low at 50%, 3 high at 75%, and 4 complete at 100%. Annual full recalculation auto-fills monopoly slots from goods at or above 25% by final percentage descending, with YAML order as the stable tie-breaker; monthly refresh updates the existing slot values but does not reselect goods. The Trade League has 1 slot by default and 2 slots once membership reaches 10; goods outside unlocked slots are not visible and provide no monopoly action benefit. `tv_trade_monopoly_<good>` is set only when the good is in an unlocked slot and has at least 50% final monopoly progress.

The monopoly/action layer generates two slot-select actions plus one fixed selected-good action set: set virtual demand or virtual production through market + value `select_trigger`s, increase/decrease/cancel either virtual action, set an embargo through market + country `select_trigger`s, cancel the embargo, and start/cancel the one active Trade League trade chain. Those fixed actions dispatch by `tv_trade_selected_good` into generated per-good script branches where needed, so the GUI does not instantiate per-good buttons. Each point of virtual demand or production locks 5% monopoly level, while each embargo locks 30%. Virtual demand uses generated `goods_demand` entries and is applied as `add_temporary_demand` with `months = -1`; set/increase/decrease/cancel actions remove and reapply it as needed, monthly monopoly maintenance removes it when the monopoly is lost, and the Trade League monthly fallback removes all virtual demand while the Grand Merchant cannot maintain monopolies. Virtual production tracks `tv_trade_virtual_supply_applied_<good>`, applies `add_goods_supply` once when active, removes it by applying a negative `add_goods_supply` amount before changing/canceling/suspending, and reapplies it when maintenance conditions recover. Embargo applies a 2-month `merchant_power_severe_penalty` through `add_merchant_power`. Action variables persist on the leader country when the monopoly is lost, but effects are applied only while `tv_trade_monopoly_<good> >= 1`. Every generated action is also registered in `tv_trade_league_list.txt` and `messagetypes.txt`. The generated Trade League custom tab shows one rectangular slot card per unlocked monopoly slot, each with a progress piechart and centered good icon, good/stage labels, final percentage and origin/transit/consumer monopoly type, plus origin, transit network, and consumer percentage rows. Selecting a filled slot refreshes the existing projected detail card and leader-only fixed action card for the selected good; the detail card keeps representative origin/transit/consumer market tables for decision support.

Six generated core law groups (`tv_govhouse_expedition_goal_law`, `tv_govhouse_governor_appointment_law`, `tv_govhouse_local_autonomy_law`, `tv_govhouse_religious_mission_law`, `tv_govhouse_frontier_military_law`, `tv_govhouse_imperial_integration_law`) each have four effect-bearing country policies. `tv_govhouse_grand_general_power_law` sits in the new `tv_govhouse_grand_general` category: Marching Orders gives newly appointed Grand Generals +10% initial loyalty, Garrison Orders makes governor task/function effects 20% stronger, and Supreme Command gives newly appointed Grand Generals -10% initial loyalty while doubling General Tactics and War Plan progress gain. Four generated Local Governor Function laws sit in `tv_govhouse_governor_functions`: the ADM/DIP/MIL function laws decide which scaled local modifiers governors apply, while `tv_govhouse_governor_duty_tendency_law` adds +0.30 task-selection weight to one of the six autonomous governor tasks. Every generated policy has a data-driven AI base vote bias from -60 to +60 in 20-point steps, favoring subject-friendly, traditional/conservative, and profit-seeking choices; when a policy is the current proposed law target (`ai_io_law_target`), the AI also adds opinion of the IO leader scaled from -200..+200 opinion to -100..+100 vote bias plus +5 vote bias per leader diplomatic reputation. All eleven laws keep `requires_vote = yes`, `has_parliament = no` hides the separate IO Parliament tab, and the IO starts with no initial policies. There is no monthly default-law backfill.

Governor's House War Tent: requires an alive Grand General. The custom War Tent tab has three units: General Tactics, War Plan, and Main Actions. On war entry, `tv_govhouse_war_entry_effect` proposes one random tactic from the eleven-tactic pool and fires `tv_govhouse.40`; the player approves or rejects it. While continuously at war, the monthly pulse has a 5% chance to propose a replacement tactic, excluding the tactic already active. Approved tactics are applied as country-scoped static modifiers and are cleared when the country leaves all wars or loses its Grand General; this approximates general tactics because the army-wide combat modifiers did not apply reliably from the stored character path. Supreme Command doubles the applied tactic modifier size, including the Capital Raid location modifier. War plans are mutually exclusive: offensive plans select a target country and, while at war with that target, scale Siege Ability by progress x 0.2% and Discipline by progress x 0.1%; defensive plans need no target and, while in any defensive war, scale Fort Defense by progress x 0.2% and Military Tactics by progress x 0.005. On war entry, applicable offensive/defensive plan bonuses are activated through `tv_govhouse.41` / `tv_govhouse.42` events instead of being granted directly by the war-entry effect. Plan progress increases monthly by Grand General adm x 0.02 up to 100, doubled under Supreme Command; aborting/changing target resets it, and peace with an offensive plan target aborts that plan.

Governor's House header action note: `tv_govhouse.gui` adds a fixed-size semi-transparent Grand General Actions card to the IO header. It displays, in priority order, the appointed Grand General's commanded army link, wartime tactic planning, peacetime subject-annexation assistance, peacetime inspections, or the no-Grand-General fallback. The peacetime annexation row is driven by the country cache `tv_govhouse_annexing_subject_active`, refreshed by `tv_govhouse_update_general_action_display_effect` from the existing monthly War Tent path so the GUI does not scan subject diplomacy directly.

8. **Global Victory Leaderboard** — Monthly per-path ranking of the top 5 countries for each victory path. `tv_update_leaderboard_effect` writes situation variables `tv_<path>_rank_1_country` through `tv_<path>_rank_5_country`; the Victory Situation page for the selected path displays those countries with flag, country name, the selected path circle icon, that path's milestone count, and that path's score text.

Governor's House eligibility/death note: new governors and Grand Generals exclude female characters and artists at the selection/action-effect entry points. There is no monthly legacy-eligibility cleanup, to keep the IO monthly path focused on active governor bonus refresh. `tv_govhouse_governor_death` is registered under `on_character_death`, fires `tv_govhouse.1`, removes the dead governor from `tv_governor_list`, clears their region modifiers, and rebuilds the display lists immediately.

Grand General training note: `tv_train_grand_general` applies the one-year `tv_grand_general_training_modifier` country modifier defined in `src/in_game/common/static_modifiers/tv_govhouse_modifiers.txt` (`court_spending_cost_modifier = 0.10`), tracks `tv_gg_training_months` on the Grand General via monthly pulse, mirrors it to the country-side GUI display variable `tv_gg_training_progress`, resolves at 12 months through `tv_training_resolve_effect`, and routes to skill gain, trait-path upgrade, injury, or death events before clearing state.

Grand General death notification note: natural Grand General deaths trigger `tv_govhouse.2` from `tv_govhouse_char_death`; training-caused deaths set `tv_gg_training_death_notified` before `kill_character = this` so the already-shown training death event is not duplicated.

Governor's House balance-of-power backlash note: `tv_govhouse_power_balance` is an IO variable in the 0..1 range and is checked monthly by `tv_govhouse_negative_monthly_pulse` (registered through `data/pulse_registry.yaml`). `tv_govhouse_power_equilibrium` recalculates monthly from a -20% base, the Grand General's share of combined ruler/general abilities, `tv_govhouse_power_consolidation`, and a subtraction equal to the mirrored Grand General loyalty value; `tv_govhouse_power_balance` then moves toward that equilibrium by up to 0.6% per month. Below 25% no backlash events fire. At 25%/50%/75% the pulse uses a single `random_list` roll with a 300 total weight so at most one backlash event can appear per month, and each eligible event has weight 1/300. Events `tv_govhouse.10`-`.13` are tier 1, `.20`-`.25` are tier 2, and `.30`-`.34` are tier 3. Every backlash event has exactly two options: accept the negative effect, or pay an alternative resource and call `tv_govhouse_reduce_power_balance_effect` to lower the balance by 5 percentage points. Tier 2 adds rare art/artist losses; tier 3 upgrades the local satisfaction loss to an area-wide effect and adds a cabinet crisis. The Governor's House GUI shows right-aligned fixed-width 25/50/75/100% markers and four warning rows below the Balance of Power bar for the <25, 25-49, 50-74, and 75-100 segments.

Grand General replacement cost note: generated Remove/Change Grand General actions call `tv_govhouse_pay_grand_general_replacement_cost_effect` before resetting `tv_govhouse_power_consolidation`; the cost is Government Power equal to `tv_govhouse_power_consolidation * 20`, so a fully consolidated Grand General costs 20 government power to remove or replace.

## IO Architecture Design Decisions

The character-led TV IOs (`tv_arts_exhibition`, `tv_diplomatic_alliance`, `tv_academy_of_sciences`, `tv_govhouse`, `tv_engineering_department`, `tv_trade_league`) share invariants that must never be broken.

### 1. leader_country = founding country, locked at creation

`on_creation = { set_leader_country = scope:actor }` runs once when the IO is created. `leader_country` is not reassigned by normal gameplay afterward; the only exception is the manual recovery action `tv_reinitialize_mod`, exposed at the top of the Victory Situation panel, which recreates missing victory-path IOs where possible and reassigns joined TV IO instances back to the current country. It controls IO permissions, `is_leader_of_international_organization` trigger, and `leader_country.modifier:` chained access in `monthly_change` scripted values.

### 2. No elections — leader_change_trigger_type = none is mandatory

All TV character-led IO type definitions keep `leader_change_trigger_type = none` and `leader_change_method = none`. These prevent the engine from reassigning `leader_country` automatically. `international_organization_chooses_new_leader` is **globally banned** from all TV IO-related code — this effect triggers the vanilla election process. Any call to it violates the no-elections design and risks silently reassigning `leader_country` to a different country.

### 3. Great person characters are independent country variables, not the vanilla ruler

The character displayed in the IO header is stored as a country variable on the `leader_country`:
- Arts Exhibition: `tv_arts_exhibition_leader_char`
- Diplomatic Alliance: `tv_diplomatic_alliance_leader_char`
- Academy of Sciences: `tv_academy_leader_char`
- Governor's House: `tv_conquest_general_char`
- Engineering Department: `tv_great_engineer_char`
- Trade League: `tv_grand_merchant_char`

These are set/cleared exclusively by the generated generic actions in `tv_io_leader_actions.txt`. The vanilla `leader_country.GetGovernment.GetRuler` and `appointed_leader.*` are different concepts and must not be used in TV IO calculations.

### 4. IO variable monthly_change uses chained character attribute access

All IO variable `monthly_change` scripted values that depend on the appointed great person must use `leader_country.var:tv_xxx_leader_char.attribute` chained access — never `appointed_leader.attribute`. Examples:
- CORRECT: `value = leader_country.var:tv_academy_leader_char.adm`
- FORBIDDEN: `value = appointed_leader.adm`

### 5. IO monthly_effect blocks are forbidden

TV international_organization type definitions must not use `monthly_effect` blocks. IO monthly effects have severe performance costs even when hidden. Keep visible typed-variable monthly arithmetic in each variable's `monthly_change`; route maintenance, completion checks, and side-effect work through registered country monthly pulses or explicit lifecycle hooks.

### 6. Header display uses blockoverride

All character-led IO panels use `blockoverride` on their header template to display the appointed character (read from the country variable via `GetPlayer.MakeScope.GetVariable(...).GetCharacter`). Never use the vanilla `GetLeaderCountry.GetGovernment.GetRulerOrRegent` accessor chain.

### 7. All TV IOs have unique = no

Each country pursuing the relevant victory path creates its own independent IO instance. The `international_organization:type_name` scope link is **invalid** for non-unique types. Always use `every_international_organizations_member_of = { limit = { international_organization_type = international_organization_type:<io_type> } ... }`.

## AI Workflow Knowledge

AI workflow state is maintained as project source, not by manual human follow-up. `scripts/ai_context.py` builds the task-scoped context from changed or explicit files, generated-file metadata, `docs/knowledge/anti_patterns.yaml`, short domain risk cards in `docs/knowledge/risk_cards/`, and content markers for `variable_map` / `global_variable_map` / `local_variable_map` usage. Current registered risk cards: `events.md`, `generic_actions.md`, `gui.md`, `international_organizations.md`, and `on_action.md`; event files route to the events card because option tooltips can pre-evaluate option effect chains, including `hidden_effect`, while IO definitions, IO laws, and country interactions that find or mutate TV IOs route to the international-organization card because IO scopes are high risk. Files containing variable-map content route to the local Variable maps documentation and prioritize the `variable_map_scope_link_used_direct_rhs` anti-pattern because direct quoted map lookups are unsafe as RHS/effect parameters. Anti-pattern entries use `detectability` to distinguish safe regex lint (`lint`), parser/semantic rules (`needs_parser`), and AI-facing warnings (`advisory`), and may name a semantic `validator` implemented in `scripts/validate.py`. `scripts/validate.py` enforces safe lint rules, emits generic-action pre-evaluation and interaction-target chooser warnings, rejects visible generic-action calls to registered hidden-only cleanup helpers, emits IO policy AI-scope warnings/errors, fails duplicate main-menu localization keys within the same language pack, rejects dotted event references whose numeric event ID is `>= 10000`, walks `monthly_country_pulse` registry callbacks and TV helper calls to require the configured one-day event delay, checks that risk cards remain registered in `ai_context.py`, checks that each TV IO type has a matching `main_menu/gfx/interface/icons/international_organizations/<io_type>.dds` GetIcon asset, rejects TV IO `monthly_effect` blocks, checks that generated main and M&T compatibility `location_window.gui` outputs match their current generators/reference inputs, and checks that TV does not stack a second direct `effect` onto hardcoded vanilla on_action hooks such as `on_annex` or `on_winning_war`. `scripts/test_lint_rules.py` runs fixture tests from `tests/fixtures/anti_patterns/<rule_id>/` for selected lint regexes.

For Engineering Department wonders, remember the noncapital building rule: EU5 building `modifier` / `raw_modifier` are local, while `capital_country_modifier` only applies country effects if the building is in the capital. Since wonders can be outside the capital, final wonder buildings keep base national/global effects in their `modifier` block only as display data; the real base country effects are generated as level-gated Country Auto modifiers, while real ritual country effects are applied as static modifiers when the selected ritual completes.

Wonder Construction random events are data-driven from `data/wonder_construction_events.yaml`. The generated outputs are `src/in_game/events/tv_wonder_construction_events.txt`, `src/in_game/common/scripted_effects/tv_wonder_construction_event_effects.txt`, `src/in_game/common/scripted_triggers/tv_wonder_construction_event_triggers.txt`, and the matching English/Simplified Chinese localization files. All generated construction-random events use the static `tv_wonder_construction.dds` event image. Hand-maintained Engineering Department wonder events also default to `tv_wonder_construction.dds`, except module synthesis uses `tv_wonder_complete.dds`, body/function/decoration module-complete split events use `tv_wonder_body.dds`, `tv_wonder_function.dds`, and `tv_wonder_decoration.dds`, and finalization/world-news events use the completed wonder's `{wonder_image_name}_cropped.dds` event-image sibling. Re-run all five `gen_tv_wonder_construction_events*` generators after changing token values, event categories, weights, eligibility rules, or flavor text.
