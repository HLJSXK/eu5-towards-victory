[SIZE=6][B]Great Project[/B][/SIZE]
[SIZE=5][B]Concept to Finalization — six stages, one national monument.[/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=4][B]Overview[/B][/SIZE]

Vanilla wonders in EU5 are decorative — a flavor tooltip, nothing more. [B]Great Project[/B] turns them into a full national undertaking: a dedicated organization to run the process, a real build pipeline with stakes at every stage, and a permanent national payoff when the doors finally open.

[SIZE=4][B]Key Features[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*] A standalone international organization, the [B]Engineering Department[/B], that runs the entire wonder-building process
[*] [B]56 generic wonders[/B] any nation can pursue, plus [B]136 historical unique wonders[/B] tied to their real-world locations — Pharos at Alexandria, Hagia Sophia at Constantinople, the Persian Qanat at Qom, and more
[*] A [B]six-stage build[/B]: Concept → Debate → Survey → Construction → Ceremony → Finalization
[*] A dedicated [B]map mode[/B] that colors every completed wonder worldwide — plan your own build, or scout other nations' finished wonders as conquest targets
[*] A companion [B]Wonders Atlas[/B] website to browse every unique wonder on a world map, outside the game
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]The Six-Stage Build[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*] [B]Concept[/B] — your Great Engineer puts forward three proposals at once, small/medium/large, based on your country's situation; accept one, refuse it for prestige, or bribe for a fresh set
[*] [B]Debate[/B] — win over nobles, burghers, and clergy to build Domestic Support (0–200) before construction can begin
[*] [B]Survey[/B] — the site is scored on Scale, Logistics, and Organization; the result permanently locks in the wonder's max level and how fast you'll ever be able to build it
[*] [B]Construction[/B] — build supporting infrastructure and pour materials into the wonder's four parts
[*] [B]Ceremony[/B] — pick from 3 styles for a generic wonder; most unique wonders instead advance automatically through a shared 8-stage ceremony, while two — the Pharos Lighthouse and Hagia Sophia — get a fully bespoke ritual; this is what actually determines the final bonuses
[*] [B]Finalization[/B] — the wonder is built, you get a national celebration buff, and it's broadcast as world news
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Compatibility & Requirements[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*] This mod [B]requires[/B] [URL='https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692202776'][B]Community Mod Framework (CMF)[/B][/URL] 2.x — it supplies the callback this mod uses to repair its own state whenever you load a save.
[*] [B]Maximum compatibility[/B]: the construction logic of this mod works on its own variables, and should be compatible with any mod.
[*] [B]UI issue[/B]: this mod modifies location_window.gui and encyclopedia_lateralview.gui. Other mods that also do this will overwrite each other. See [URL='?']Great Project - Compatibility Submods[/URL] for maintained patches.
[/LIST]
