# Steam Workshop Page — English (concise)

**Title:** Towards Victory - Great Project

---

Vanilla wonders in EU5 are decorative — a flavor tooltip, nothing more. **Great Project** turns them into a full national undertaking.

**What you get:**
- A standalone international organization, the **Engineering Department**, that runs the entire wonder-building process
- **54 generic wonders** any nation can pursue, plus **123 historical unique wonders** tied to their real-world locations (Pharos at Alexandria, Hagia Sophia at Constantinople, the Persian Qanat at Qom, and more)
- A **six-stage build**: Concept → Debate → Survey → Construction → Ceremony → Finalization — propose a wonder, win domestic support, survey the site, build it up, and choose the ceremony that defines its final effect
- A companion **Wonders Atlas** website to browse every unique wonder on a world map

**Requirements:** Community Mod Framework 2.x. Fully standalone — no dependency on any other mod.

Works on its own, or alongside the main *Towards Victory* mod.
