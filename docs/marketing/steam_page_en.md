[h1]Overview[/h1]

Vanilla wonders in EU5 are decorative — a flavor tooltip, nothing more.
[b]Great Project[/b] turns them into a full national undertaking: a dedicated
organization to run the process, a real build pipeline with stakes at every
stage, and a permanent national payoff when the doors finally open.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Core Features[/h1]

[img]https://.../engineering_department_panel.png[/img]
[i]The Engineering Department panel — where the whole build is tracked.[/i]

[list]
[*] A standalone international organization, the [b]Engineering Department[/b], that runs the entire wonder-building process — non-unique, never triggers vanilla IO elections, led by an appointed Great Engineer rather than your ruler
[*] [b]54 generic wonders[/b] any nation can pursue, plus [b]123 historical unique wonders[/b] tied to their real-world locations — Pharos at Alexandria, Hagia Sophia at Constantinople, the Persian Qanat at Qom, and more
[*] A [b]six-stage build[/b]: Concept → Debate → Survey → Construction → Ceremony → Finalization
[*] A companion [b]Wonders Atlas[/b] website to browse every unique wonder on a world map, outside the game
[/list]

[img]https://.../construction_stage.png[/img]
[i]Construction — pour materials into the foundation, body, function, and decoration.[/i]

[h2]The six stages[/h2]
[olist]
[*] [b]Concept[/b] — your Great Engineer proposes a wonder based on your country's situation; accept it, refuse it for prestige, or bribe for a different one
[*] [b]Debate[/b] — win over nobles, burghers, and clergy to build domestic support before construction can begin
[*] [b]Survey[/b] — the site is scored on Scale, Logistics, and Organization, permanently locking in the wonder's max level, cost, and build time
[*] [b]Construction[/b] — build supporting infrastructure and pour materials into the wonder's four parts
[*] [b]Ceremony[/b] — pick from 3 styles for a generic wonder, or a bespoke history-driven ceremony for a unique one; this is what actually determines the final bonuses
[*] [b]Finalization[/b] — the wonder is built, you get a national celebration buff, and it's broadcast as world news
[/olist]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

[list]
[*] [b]Requires:[/b] Community Mod Framework (CMF) 2.x
[*] [b]Fully standalone[/b] — no dependency on any other mod, including our main [i]Towards Victory[/i] mod
[*] Works on its own, or alongside [i]Towards Victory[/i]: the main mod depends on Great Project (not the other way around) and calls into it for one of its victory milestones
[/list]
